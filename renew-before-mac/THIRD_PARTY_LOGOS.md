# Service names and marks

No third-party service-logo image binaries are included in this public baseline. The alias catalog is presentation metadata, not an integration or endorsement.

Service names and marks belong to their owners. The source MIT license grants no rights to those marks. Existing components use initials for unavailable images.

Historical Simple Icons notices remain in `Core/Resources/service-logo-notices.txt` for provenance. This does not mean original directory images are included or cleared. See [the public profile](Docs/SERVICE_LOGOS.md).
