Log In to Merchant Site

Instructions:

1. Open the merchant site (opens in new tab)

2. Enter your email and password

3. Complete 2FA if prompted

3
Navigate to Account Settings

Find the cancellation option:

Look for one of these:

  • "Manage Subscription"
  • "Account Settings"
  • "Cancel Subscription" (might be hidden or small)
Dark Pattern Alert: This merchant hides the cancel link. Look for small text that says "Need to cancel your subscription?"
4
Handle Retention Offers

They may try to keep you:

  • Discount offers (e.g., "50% off for 3 months!")
  • "Limited time" urgency
  • Emotional appeals

Your Decision Required

Did they show you a retention offer?

5
Confirm Cancellation

Final confirmation:

They may use confirmshaming — guilt-inducing button text like:

  • "Yes, I want to lose all these benefits"
  • "No, I don't want to save money"

It's OK to proceed. This is a dark pattern. Your decision is valid.

Dark Pattern: Confirmshaming makes you feel bad for cancelling. Ignore it.
