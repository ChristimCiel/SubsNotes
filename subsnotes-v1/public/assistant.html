🚀 Getting Started with SubsNotes

Your AI-powered subscription manager

💡 What SubsNotes Does

Reduces decision fatigue by handling subscription management automatically within your budget. Your AI agent detects subscriptions, recommends actions, and executes with your explicit approval.

1

Set Your Monthly Budget

Tell SubsNotes how much you want to spend on subscriptions each month. The system tracks allocation, spend, and remaining capacity.

No money is held or stored. This is planning only. Licensed-partner wallets coming in future versions.

2

Connect Your Local AI Agent

Your local AI agent (Claude Desktop, Cursor skill, or MCP) reads receipts from your email or files locally and sends only a cryptographically signed summary.

🔒 Security: Why This Is Safe

  • Local processing: Receipt documents never leave your device
  • Ed25519 signatures: Server verifies authenticity, rejects tampering
  • Field allowlist: Only merchant, amount, currency, date accepted
  • Prompt injection detection: Malicious payloads automatically rejected

What gets sent:

{
  "merchant": "Netflix",
  "amount": 15.99,
  "currency": "USD",
  "date": "2026-11-25",
  "signature": "Ed25519-signature",
  "publicKey": "your-public-key"
}

What NEVER gets sent: Full receipts, credit card numbers, passwords, personal details.

3

Review AI Recommendations

SubsNotes analyzes each subscription and recommends: Keep, Downgrade, or Cancel

AI detects:

  • 📈 Price increases (alerts when > 10%)
  • ⏰ Free trials expiring soon (7-day warning)
  • 💤 Unused services (60+ days inactive)
  • 🔁 Duplicate subscriptions in same category
  • 💰 Budget impact (flags subscriptions > 25% of budget)
4

Approve Actions via Mandates

Before ANY action, you must approve an explicit mandate specifying:

  • Merchant: Which service (e.g., "Netflix")
  • Action: keep, downgrade, or cancel
  • Scope: single, recurring, or category
  • Max amount: Dollar limit
  • Expiry: When mandate expires

All mandates are logged in an append-only audit trail.

5

AI Agent Executes (3 Tiers)

Execution happens at different levels based on the merchant:

  • T0 Deep Links - Opens merchant's cancellation page
    Supported: Apple App Store, Google Play, LINE Pay, 全支付 (JKO Pay), 街口支付 (Jkopay)
  • T1 Supervised - Step-by-step guidance while you do it
    AI guides you through each step, waits for confirmation
  • T2 Autonomous - AI handles it completely
    Playwright automation, handles dark patterns (retention offers, confirmshaming)
    ⚠️ Login and 2FA are always user-present (no stored passwords)
6

Verify & Record

After execution, the AI:

  • ✅ Verifies cancellation from page state or confirmation email
  • 📸 Captures evidence (confirmation ID, screenshot)
  • 📝 Records in audit log with timestamp
  • 💬 Notifies you of completion

🛡️ Security & Privacy Summary

  • Threat Model: Protects against receipt tampering, prompt injection, and unauthorized actions
  • Known Limits: Apple/Google Play are T0 only (deep-link), some merchants have dark patterns
  • Mitigations: Ed25519 signatures, field allowlist, prompt detection, mandate approval, audit log
  • No Credentials Stored: Login and 2FA always require you to be present
