How SubsNotes Works

The Closed Loop

  1. Detect: AI reads receipts (locally) and builds your subscription list
  2. Judge: AI evaluates against your budget and usage patterns
  3. Recommend: AI suggests keep/downgrade/cancel with clear reasons
  4. Approve: You approve actions via explicit mandates
  5. Execute: AI agent performs the action within mandate limits
  6. Verify: AI confirms completion and records evidence

Security & Privacy

  • Your local AI agent reads receipts locally and sends only signed summaries
  • Ed25519 signatures prevent tampering and prompt injection
  • No passwords stored; login and 2FA are user-present
  • Append-only audit log tracks all actions

Execution Tiers

  • T0: Deep links (Apple App Store, Google Play, LINE Pay, 全支付 JKO Pay, 街口支付 Jkopay)
  • T1: Supervised step-by-step guidance
  • T2: Autonomous execution (demo only in this MVP)

Known Limitations

  • Apple/Google Play subscriptions are deep-link only
  • Some merchants have dark patterns (retention offers, hidden buttons)
  • All data in this MVP is fictional/virtual
